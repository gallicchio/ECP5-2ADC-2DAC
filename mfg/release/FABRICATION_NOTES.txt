NOTES
Board: 158 x 119 mm, 6 layers, 1.6 mm, JLCPCB stackup JLC06161H-7628 (1 oz outer, 0.5 oz inner, 7628 prepreg).
Surface finish: ENIG (there is a 0.8 mm pitch BGA).  Solder mask green, silkscreen white.
Via-in-pad under the BGA: all 0.3 mm / 0.15 mm vias must be epoxy filled and capped (JLCPCB's default for 6+ layers; tick it).
The FPGA's 100 nF decoupling sits on the BACK directly under the BGA, each 0402 on two adjacent ball vias (0.8 mm pitch): those vias must be capped on the back too (the filled-and-capped option covers both sides).
Enclosure: Hammond 1455Q (125 x 51.5 mm extrusion, 160 or 220 mm long); the board's long edges slide into its PCB slots, so nothing sits within 3.5 mm of them.  SMAs through the left end plate, all other connectors through the right one.
Photodiode D103 uses ours:VBPW34S_K1_A2 (LCSC's footprint with pads renumbered so pad 1 is the cathode); its orientation on the board is the same as JLCPCB's library part, pad 1 of theirs (the anode) on the -5 V side.  Check it in the placement preview.
The mini-HDMI jack is wired with type C pin numbering (shield first in each pair, DDC ground 13, CEC 14); its four shell tabs (pads 20-23) are grounded.
Minimum track/space on the board: 0.100 mm (inside the BGA fan-out; 0.127 mm elsewhere).  Via sizes: 0.3/0.15, 0.45/0.25.  Smallest drill 0.15 mm.
Fiducials: three 1 mm copper dots in 2 mm mask openings, front and back, at (25.5, 12.5), (120.0, 7.5), (146.5, 113.0) mm from the board's top-left corner (x right, y down); JLCPCB adds its own on the panel rails as well.
Layer markers: the copper layer numbers 1-6, each in copper on its own layer, in a row at the top edge (x 70-82, y 1.9 mm, inside the extrusion's slot band): hold the bare board against the light to check the layer order.
Order number: the back silkscreen carries JLCJLCJLCJLC at the top edge (x 95, y 1.9 mm, in the slot band).  On the order form choose 'Remove order number: Specify a location'; JLCPCB prints its number there, hidden by the extrusion.
Impedance: ask for controlled impedance with the JLC06161H-7628 calculator: 50 ohm single-ended (SMA runs, 0.35 mm drawn) and 100 ohm differential (Ethernet MDI, 0.2 mm / 0.2 mm drawn), referenced to In1.
The shipped board file holds 361 filled zone polygons; DRC 0 errors / 0 unconnected (build/drc.json).

Assembly (JLCPCB Standard, SMT both sides + through-hole on top): 446 SMT placements and 28 through-hole parts on 95 BOM lines, 49 of them Extended parts (each carries a one-off loading fee).
Through-hole, fitted by JLCPCB (Jason, 2026-10-06): the six SMA jacks, the RJ45, the DIP switch, the 2.54 mm headers (ordered as 1x40 / 2x40 strips the assembler snaps to length, as on the GPSDO board) and the two Pmod sockets.  146 through-hole joints incl. the USB-C shells.
Upload: ECP5-2ADC-2DAC_bom.csv and ECP5-2ADC-2DAC_cpl_jlcpcb_upload.csv (JLCPCB's conventions: the bottom-side mirror, rotations read in previews or predicted from their drawings, the USB-C moved to their origin); ECP5-2ADC-2DAC_cpl_kicad_native.csv is KiCad's own numbers for reference.
THEN READ THE PLACEMENT PREVIEW against PLACEMENT_CHECKS.txt: 81 placements of 35 part numbers have a predicted or unverified rotation; the bottom side's 7 polarized parts hardest of all (this board stores its back-side pads mirrored left-right, checked on C_0402_1005Metric).
Hand solder (hand_solder_bom.csv): nothing -- every part is assembled by JLCPCB; the file is its header alone.
Coordinates: the board's corner is at (25, 25) mm on KiCad's page (pcb/place.py OX, OY); the gerbers, the drill file and the CPL all use that frame, so they agree.
End plates: end_panel_left/right.svg and .dxf (1:1, viewed from outside) with every connector's cutout; end_panels.txt lists them.  MEASURE which slot the board rides in and set PCB_TOP_ABOVE_FLOOR in mfg/end_panels.py before milling.
